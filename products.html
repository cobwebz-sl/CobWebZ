How it works

From template to your site.

A template gets you live sooner than a design from scratch. It's still built properly, by hand, for you.

  1. Pick a design

    Open the live previews and click around. Tell us which one you like, and what your business needs it to do.

  2. We make it yours

    Your logo, colours, fonts, words and photos replace the demo content. Extra pages and features are quoted up front.

  3. Launch and look after it

    It goes live on your domain with SSL. After that, we can keep it updated and backed up on a monthly plan.

Prices in Sri Lankan rupees, starting at the package shown on each card. Domain, hosting and third-party fees are billed by their providers. Want something original? Estimate a custom site.
