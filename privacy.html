Privacy

Privacy notice, in plain words.

What this website does with your data (very little), and what we do with the details you send us.

Who we are

CobWebZ is a web design and development studio based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. For anything about your data, email cobwebzsl@gmail.com or call +94 71 907 0320.

What this website collects

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ad trackers. The site doesn't set cookies or run any tracking or analytics code.
  • No third-party scripts. Every file, including the fonts, comes from our own server, and the browser enforces that with a content security policy.
  • One setting, kept on your device. If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers the choice locally. It's never sent to us.
  • The live checks stay in your browser. The speed and security checks on the homepage ask our own server for this page and measure the answer in your browser. The results aren't sent anywhere.
  • Our host. The site is served by Cloudflare, which handles basic request data (such as your IP address) to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks, under its own privacy policy.

When you send an enquiry

The enquiry form doesn't send anything by itself. Pressing its button opens WhatsApp with your message written out, and it's only sent if you press Send there, after which WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply. If you email or call us instead, we receive what you send.

We use your details only to reply to you and, if we work together, to run your project. We don't sell them, share them for marketing or add you to a mailing list.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages only as long as we need them to reply, and to run and support any project that follows.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, at any time. Email cobwebzsl@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date below changes with it.
