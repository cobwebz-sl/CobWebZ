MUTE No JavaScript required.

An online store for a small clothing label, built without a single line of JavaScript. Shoppers pick a tone, quick-add from the range and open a full product page with a size guide.

Overview

A store without a single script.

MUTE is a fictional label making heavyweight hoodies in muted tones. We designed the shop around its one big idea, colour, and built every interaction in HTML and CSS alone. There is no JavaScript on the page at all.

Objective

Rich, fast and hard to break.

Online stores often load a lot of code before you see a single product. MUTE set out to show that a store can still feel rich (colour picking, quick add, a bag, product pages) with nothing to download and run first, and nothing to break if scripts are blocked.

Design approach

Let the colour talk.

A calm, warm palette and generous white space keep the attention on the product. Archivo, a variable typeface, stretches wide for headlines and narrows for labels. Every hoodie is shown in its actual tone, so the colour picker shows what you'd receive.

  • #D6CBBB · Sand
  • #F5F2ED · Bone
  • #62766F · Sage
  • #2F3135 · Charcoal
  • #141414 · Ink

Technologies

What it's built with.

  • HTML formsRadio buttons hold the state: the chosen tone, size, filter and gallery view
  • CSS :has()Restyles the page from that state: the hero recolours and the range filters itself
  • Popover APIThe bag, search, size guide and image zoom open as popovers, with no script
  • View transitionsA smooth hand-over between the store and the product page
  • Tailwind CSSUtility styles, compiled to one small stylesheet per page
  • PythonAn image script cut out the product photos and recoloured them into every tone

Main features

What it does.

Pick your tone

Choose a colour in the hero, and the product and its background follow, using radio buttons and :has().

Shop by fit

Filter the range by style with plain radio buttons; the grid updates instantly.

Quick add and the bag

Add a hoodie straight from the grid; the bag slides in as a native popover.

A full product page

Gallery views, colours and sizes, zoom, reviews and detail tabs.

A size guide in cm or inches

Opens in a popover, with a unit switch that converts the whole table.

Entrances and page transitions

Panels animate in with @starting-style, and view transitions carry you between pages.

MUTE is a concept project: a fictional label we designed and built ourselves. As a demo, its bag doesn't take payments. A live store adds a back end for products and orders, and a payment gateway (see Dynamic Applications under Pricing).
