Black. Shown two ways.

A dark, editorial showcase for a photography and sound collective that works two ways: as a cinematic story you scroll, or as a slide deck you can present from.

Overview

A gallery opening, online.

Black. is a fictional collective of photographers and sound designers. Its site had to feel like walking into a gallery: dark, slow and confident, with the work given room to breathe.

Objective

One site, two ways to show it.

Creative studios show their work in two places: on the web, to people who find them, and in meetings, to people they're pitching. Black. does both from the same content, with a cinematic scroll story for visitors and a full-screen deck for presenting.

Design approach

Monochrome, with weight.

Near-black backgrounds and high-contrast black-and-white photography. Syne sets the display type, Space Grotesk and Plus Jakarta Sans the text, and Italiana adds an editorial accent. Motion is deliberate (parallax, image wipes, a pinned gallery), and sound is synthesised in the browser and entirely optional.

Technologies

What it's built with.

  • HTML & Tailwind CSSLayout and type, with the styles compiled ahead of time
  • JavaScriptThe two view modes, the deck, the lightboxes, the cursor and the reveals
  • Web Audio APIInterface sounds generated on the fly, so there are no audio files to download
  • Lucide iconsLine icons, bundled with the site rather than loaded from elsewhere

Main features

What it does.

Scroll story or slide deck

Switch between a cinematic scroll and a full-screen deck. The deck is for presenting on larger screens; phones get the story.

A deck you can present from

Arrow keys, a progress bar and dots, auto-play, swipe on touch screens, and an index grid to jump to any slide.

Accessible lightboxes

Projects open in dialogs that keep keyboard focus inside, make the page behind inert, and close with Escape.

Sound, if you want it

Interface sounds are synthesised with the Web Audio API. They stay off until a visitor turns them on.

Editorial motion

Parallax, image wipes, a pinned gallery, scrambled-text reveals and numbers that count up.

An enquiry form

A dialog form with inline validation, so the site works as a first contact, not just a gallery.

Black. is a concept project: a fictional collective we designed and built ourselves. It isn't a client commission, and the people, projects and figures on it are made up for the demo.
