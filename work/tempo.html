Tempo Pick a class. Pick your spot.

A bold site for a movement studio with four disciplines. Members pick a class from the weekly schedule, choose their exact spot in the room and just show up.

Overview

A studio with no front desk.

Tempo is a fictional movement studio in Colombo offering Reformer, Ride, Strength and Flow. Its site does the front desk's job: the schedule, booking, pricing and a countdown to your next class all live on one page.

Objective

From scrolling to booked in a minute.

Studio sites often send members off to a separate booking app. Tempo keeps it in one place: see what's on this week, filter by discipline and time of day, take a spot and see how many are left. The first class is free, to remove the last excuse.

Design approach

Loud, like the playlists.

Chalk white and near-black, with an acid-lime accent and a colour for each discipline. Archivo sets heavy, all-caps headlines, and JetBrains Mono gives the times, spots and prices the look of a scoreboard.

  • #0B0B0B · Black
  • #ECEBE6 · Chalk
  • #CCFF00 · Lime
  • #FF4D1F · Flame
  • #7A5CFF · Violet
  • #00C2A8 · Teal

Technologies

What it's built with.

  • Next.js & ReactThe schedule, filters and booking flow as React components
  • Tailwind CSSUtility styles, compiled to one small stylesheet
  • Three.jsThe 3D scene in the hero, drawn with WebGL
  • Web Audio APIAn optional click track for the tempo ring, generated in the browser
  • Motion & LenisSnappy transitions and smooth scrolling
  • localStorageBookings are kept in the browser, so My Classes remembers them

Main features

What it does.

A live weekly schedule

Seven days at a glance. Filter by discipline and time of day, and see the spots left in every class.

Pick your spot

Choose your exact place in the room for each class, then find it under My Classes.

Your next class, counting down

The header shows your next class and counts down the time until it starts.

Tap to set the tempo

Tap the ring to set the beats per minute. It pulses in time, with sound if you switch it on.

Four disciplines

Reformer, Ride, Strength and Flow, each with its intensity and typical calories.

Memberships and class packs

Switch between the two ways to pay. Every plan starts with a free class.

Tempo is a concept project: a fictional studio we designed and built ourselves. Its bookings are stored only in your browser, and the coaches, reviews and figures on it are made up. A live studio adds accounts, a booking back end and payments (see Web Application under Pricing).
