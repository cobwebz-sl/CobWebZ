Wren Play it before you buy it.

A launch site for a small eight-voice synthesiser. The instrument on the page really plays: use your mouse, your keyboard or a MIDI controller, then take it apart and choose your finish.

Overview

A synth you can hear on the page.

Wren W-25 is a fictional 25-key synthesiser with a speaker, a battery and a patch bay built in. Instead of describing how it sounds, the site lets you play it. Its sound engine runs in the browser, and the 3D instrument beside the text plays when you tap its keys.

Objective

Let the product sell itself.

People buy instruments on sound and feel, and both are hard to get across on a screen. Wren puts the instrument in the visitor's hands first: six presets, eight knobs and a patch bay to play with, before any specs or prices.

Design approach

Warm metal and wood.

A bone-coloured background and warm near-black, with the rust orange of Wren's signature finish. Mona Sans sets the headlines, and Martian Mono gives the labels and specs the look of a printed front panel.

Technologies

What it's built with.

  • Next.js & ReactThe page, the synth's controls and the bag as React components
  • Three.jsThe 3D synth, modelled in Blender, which comes apart layer by layer as you scroll
  • Web Audio APIAn eight-voice sound engine with oscillators, a filter, envelopes, an LFO and effects
  • Web MIDI APIPlug in a MIDI keyboard and play Wren from real keys
  • Motion & LenisSmooth scrolling and transitions that keep the 3D scenes in step
  • Tailwind CSSUtility styles, compiled to one small stylesheet

Main features

What it does.

Play it here

Click the keys or type on your keyboard, switch between six presets, or connect a MIDI controller.

Eight knobs, no menus

Cutoff, resonance, LFO rate, space and the envelope all change the sound as you turn them.

A working patch bay

Drag a cable to route the LFO, envelope or a random source to the filter, pitch or volume, and listen.

Taken apart, layer by layer

Scroll and the 3D synth separates into its eight parts, from the knobs to the wooden cheeks.

Choose your finish

Four colours, three woods and two knob colours, with the price updating as you choose.

Sound packs to try first

Preview each pack on the same engine before adding it to the bag.

Wren is a concept project: a fictional instrument we designed and built ourselves. The synth on the page really plays, but the product, prices, reviews and specifications are made up, and the bag doesn't take payments. A live store adds a back end for products and orders, and a payment gateway (see Web Application under Pricing).
