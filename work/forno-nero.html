Forno Nero Straight from the oven.

A wood-fired pizzeria with an online menu, a build-your-own pizza tool and table booking. Its opening hours drive the whole site, so it always knows whether the kitchen is open.

Overview

A pizzeria that takes orders.

Forno Nero is a fictional Neapolitan pizzeria in Brooklyn. Its site does what a restaurant's website is for: it shows the menu, takes the order and fills the tables, with the heat of a wood-fired oven in every section.

Objective

Order, build or book, without a phone call.

Many restaurant sites are a PDF menu and a phone number. Forno Nero shows that a small restaurant can take orders, sell its weekly deal and fill tables from its own site, in a few taps on a phone.

Design approach

Dark, warm and a little loud.

Near-black backgrounds let the food photography glow, and a fire gradient from amber to pink runs through the buttons and highlights. Bricolage Grotesque sets the chunky headlines, DM Sans the text, and Caveat adds handwritten notes, like a chalkboard special.

Technologies

What it's built with.

  • HTML & CSSHand-written layout and styles, with every colour and font set once as a token
  • JavaScriptThe menu, cart, pizza builder, countdown, reviews slider and gallery, with no framework
  • localStorageKeeps the cart in the browser, so an order survives a refresh
  • Dialog elementThe photo gallery opens in a native, keyboard-friendly lightbox
  • Schema.org dataRestaurant details, address and hours marked up for search engines
  • WebP imagesEvery photo compressed and lazy-loaded to keep the page quick

Main features

What it does.

A menu with a cart

Five categories with dietary badges. Tap + on any dish and the cart keeps count.

Build your own pizza

Pick a size, crust, sauce and toppings, and watch the pizza and its price update as you go.

Always knows if it's open

One set of opening hours drives the open or closed badge, the hours list and the booking form.

Booking that checks the hours

Choose a date, time, party size and occasion. The form won't take a booking on a day the kitchen is closed.

A deal with a deadline

The weekly Family Feast counts down to Sunday midnight and shows what you save.

Reviews, gallery and a map

A reviews slider, a photo gallery that opens in a lightbox, and a map to find the door.

Forno Nero is a concept project: a fictional pizzeria we designed and built ourselves. Its checkout, booking and newsletter forms are demos that only show a confirmation, and the reviews and figures on it are made up. A live restaurant connects them to its ordering, booking and email services (see Web Application under Pricing).
