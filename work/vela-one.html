Vela One Turned from titanium.

A launch page for a pair of titanium headphones that tells the product's story as you scroll: the ear cup comes apart layer by layer, the noise readout drops as you switch modes, and the frequency curve draws itself.

Overview

A product film you can scroll.

Vela One is a fictional pair of titanium headphones. We designed and built its launch page to show how one scrolling page can do the job of a product video: every scene is driven by the scroll, and every claim on the page has something to look at or play with.

Objective

Make a technical product feel tactile.

Visitors should understand the materials, the driver and the battery life without reading a spec sheet first, and arrive at the pre-order with the details already in hand. The page also had to work just as well for people who switch animation off.

Design approach

Quiet colour, loud detail.

A near-monochrome palette lets the product carry the colour. Inter Tight sets the headlines and IBM Plex Mono the technical labels, so specifications read like engineering notes. Each section answers one question (what it's made of, how it sounds, how long it lasts) and hands over to the next as you scroll.

Technologies

What it's built with.

  • HTML & CSSLayout, type, and a finished, still version of every scene for reduced motion
  • JavaScriptThe pre-order bag, the listening-mode switch and the custom cursor
  • GSAP & ScrollTriggerPinned scenes, scrubbed to the scroll position
  • Canvas 2DA pointer-reactive sound field that calms down in Quiet mode
  • SVGThe frequency response curve

Main features

What it does.

Scroll-driven scenes

Each section pins in place while its scene plays, then hands over to the next. Scrolling back plays it in reverse.

An ear cup, taken apart

Scroll to separate the ear cup into its seven layers, each labelled, from the ceramic outer plate to the cushion.

Quiet and Ambient modes

A switch animates the outside-noise readout between −32 dB and −6 dB, and the sound field calms or wakes to match.

A curve that draws itself

The frequency response traces itself across 20 Hz to 20 kHz as you reach it.

A pre-order bag

Add Vela One and its companions (Buds, Field, Dock and Link), and the bag counts as you go.

Motion is optional

With reduced motion switched on, every scene shows its finished state instead of animating.

Vela One is a concept project: a fictional brand we designed and built ourselves to show what a launch page can do. It isn't a client commission, and the product, prices and specifications on it are made up for the demo.
