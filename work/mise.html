Mise Order from the table.

A neighbourhood kitchen where guests scan the QR code on their table and order and pay from their phone. No app, no login and no waiting to catch someone's eye.

Overview

The menu comes to you.

Mise is a fictional neighbourhood kitchen in Colombo that cooks over charcoal. Every table has a QR code. Scanning it opens the menu with that table already set, so guests can order, pay and order again without waiting for a server.

Objective

Three taps from hungry to happy.

QR menus are often a PDF that's hard to read on a phone. Mise shows what one can be instead: a menu made for the phone, where ordering, paying and following your order all happen on one page, and the place still feels like a restaurant.

Design approach

Warm paper, charcoal and chilli.

A cream, paper-like background and warm black ink keep it feeling like a printed menu. Fraunces, a soft serif, sets the dish names, with Hanken Grotesk for prices and details. Chilli red marks what you can tap, and mustard yellow lights up tonight's specials.

  • #F4EEE4 · Paper
  • #1D1A16 · Ink
  • #D23B1E · Chilli
  • #F1C04C · Mustard
  • #4B5A2B · Olive

Technologies

What it's built with.

  • Next.js & ReactThe menu, bag and checkout as React components, rendered ahead for a fast first load
  • Three.jsThe signature ramen as a real 3D model that you lift apart by scrolling
  • Tailwind CSSUtility styles, compiled to one small stylesheet
  • MotionSpringy transitions for the bag, dish sheets and order status
  • LenisSmooth scrolling that keeps the 3D scene in step with the page
  • VercelHosting on a global network, so the menu opens quickly on any table

Main features

What it does.

Your table, already set

Scanning the QR code opens the menu with the table number filled in, and a greeting to match.

Tweak it, then add it

Dishes come with options and a box for kitchen notes, and the bag keeps a running total.

A menu that follows you

The category bar tracks your scroll, and dietary filters show vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free or spicy dishes.

Tonight's specials

A carousel of the chef's picks, each one a tap away from your bag.

The signature dish in 3D

Ramen No. 9 lifts apart as you scroll, showing the broth, egg, chashu and noodles, one layer at a time.

Pay and follow your order

A simulated checkout with tipping, then a live status as the order moves from the kitchen to your table.

Mise is a concept project: a fictional restaurant we designed and built ourselves. Its payments are simulated and nothing is charged. A live restaurant connects it to a payment gateway and a kitchen screen or point-of-sale system (see Dynamic Applications under Pricing).
