LS Nails Studio — nail salon in Negombo

Client project Nail salon

LS Nails

A website for a nail and lash studio in Negombo. It shows off the studio's work, answers what people ask before a first visit, and turns a booking request into a WhatsApp message the studio can reply to straight away.

  • Nail, lash and waxing services, each a tap from booking
  • Portfolio in three styles: Classic, Graphic and Maximalist
  • Pages and FAQ written for people searching for a nail salon in Negombo
  • Booking form that opens WhatsApp with the request filled in

CUBE — Three instruments. One heartbeat.

Client project Live music

CUBE

  • Open mic nights, private and corporate events, and weddings
